How to read this. Agreement comes first: several of the most profitable wallets independently holding the same side, with real money behind it. vs entry compares today's price to what they paid — near zero is the sweet spot. A large positive number means the price fell away from them, which usually means the market learned something they hadn't, so treat it as a warning rather than a discount. A large negative number means the move already happened and you would be buying in late.
What this cannot tell you. These wallets are ranked by raw profit, which tracks bankroll size as much as skill, and a 30-day window cannot separate skill from luck. Copying a position always means entering at a worse price than they got, and you cannot see their hedges, their bankroll, or why they took the bet. This is a research shortlist, not advice.
